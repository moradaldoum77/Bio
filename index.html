My Projects

Some of the software I've built while studying Computer Science.

Veyra: Smart City Digital Twin

An interactive 3D digital twin of a smart city, built with Three.js. Rotate, zoom and click through a low-poly city with skyscrapers, parks, a harbor with bridges and boats, suburbs and mountains. Switch between traffic, energy, environment, transit and infrastructure layers that visibly change the city. A simulation engine with defined formulas connects every control to live metrics, charts and alerts, and a scenario page lets you apply presets and compare results against a saved baseline. All data is simulated.

BroBot

A joke virtual assistant that gives confidently wrong answers and calls you "bro" way too much, built for the Stupid Hackathon at Western University. It uses OpenAI's GPT-4o to generate the unhelpful replies and ElevenLabs text-to-speech to speak them out loud. A video avatar made from short clips reacts with different emotions, and the page tracks a running "bro" count, shows a mood meter, and includes a draggable Brainrot Mode window.

Virtual Pet Game

A desktop pet-care game built in Java with Swing. Choose and name a pet, then keep it healthy by feeding it, putting it to sleep, taking it to the vet, exercising it, and giving it gifts. Bars track its health, sleepiness, hunger and happiness, and it can die if you neglect it. The game also has a shop, food and gift inventories, a click-the-poop mini-game that earns coins, and parental controls for playtime limits and reviving a pet. It includes a built-in tutorial that walks through every screen.
